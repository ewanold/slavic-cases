# German simple

120 original, AI-assisted draft questions: four cases × two tracks × 15 questions.
The YAML files are authoritative and can be edited directly. All sentences are
original MIT material; linked grammar references provide guidance, not copied
exercises. Independent linguistic review is pending.

## What this pack teaches

- **Nominative:** subjects in ordinary sentences.
- **Accusative:** direct objects, with masculine article changes.
- **Dative:** verbs such as helfen, danken and antworten, and mit; one location
  example uses in with wohnen, not motion toward a destination.
- **Genitive:** possession and relationships, avoiding contentious colloquial
  preposition choices. Some article-free genitive examples are formal usage.
- **Nouns:** definite articles, masculine/feminine/neuter nouns, weak masculine
  Student/Nachbar, singular genitive endings and plural dative -n.
- **Adjectives/numerals:** complete phrases with definite articles (weak),
  indefinite articles (mixed), no article (strong), cardinal and ordinal numbers.

Answers include the complete phrase, not just an adjective: e.g. `dem kleinen Kind`.
Hints specify the article pattern, noun, gender and number without inflecting the
noun. German has repeated forms across cases; options are deduplicated, with
incorrect article/ending combinations used where the paradigm offers fewer than
four distinct forms. Judge the target case in its sentence, not from an ending
alone. In Guess the case, the arrow identifies the phrase being tested.

English and German native texts are supplied. When the GUI and learning language
are both German, the app hides the German translation because it contains the
answer. The neutral German prompt and dictionary cues remain visible.

## Review and expansion

Check that precisely one option fits the requested case, number, gender and article
pattern. Check whole sentences, translations and distractor quality. Keep IDs stable.
For feedback or corrections, follow the repository README's sample contribution
instructions. Future packs can separate prepositions, article patterns and plural
practice into deep topics; this starter does not claim complete German grammar
coverage. Pronouns, possessives, contracted prepositions and systematic motion vs
location exercises are not yet included.

The reusable exporter `scripts/create-german-samples.py --output /new/directory`
recreates the initial drafts and refuses to overwrite existing YAML. It does not
replace subsequent contributor edits.
